About

Connecting molecular physics to real-world problems.

14peer-reviewed and research publications
Honors & fellowshipsRiki Kobayashi Prize · Future Faculty Fellowship · Ken Kennedy Institute Fellowship

I am a Postdoctoral Research Associate in the Chemical Separations Group at Oak Ridge National Laboratory (ORNL). My current research focuses on the molecular-level thermodynamics and properties of molten salts relevant to nuclear fission and fusion technologies.

My work combines statistical mechanics, density functional theory, molecular dynamics, free-energy methods, and machine-learning interatomic potentials to connect molecular interactions with experimentally measurable behavior.

My broader research experience spans molecular biophysics and medical imaging—including NMR relaxation and MRI contrast agents—to ionic systems, molten salts, nuclear energy, and emerging quantum-computing approaches for molecular simulation.

Research

Research directions

A common thread across my work is using molecular-level simulation and statistical mechanics to explain and predict macroscopic behavior.

Molecular biophysics & medicine

Statistical Mechanics, NMR & Molecular Biophysics

Molecular theories and simulations of NMR relaxation, complex fluids, and gadolinium-based MRI contrast agents, connecting microscopic molecular motion to experimentally measurable relaxation behavior.

Nuclear materials

Molten Salts & Nuclear Energy

Atomic-scale structure, thermodynamics, transport, and speciation in molten salts relevant to nuclear fission, fusion, and chemical separations.

Simulation & AI

Machine-Learning Interatomic Potentials

Physics-informed machine-learning potentials that extend quantum-mechanical accuracy to the length and time scales required for statistically converged molecular simulations.

Quantum chemistry

Quantum Computing & Electronic Structure

Quantum-classical approaches for challenging electronic-structure problems, including molten salts relevant to tritium chemistry and future fusion blanket materials.
